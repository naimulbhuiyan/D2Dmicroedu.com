Frequently asked questions

Plain answers to the questions members ask most. Still stuck? Contact microedu — the number and email are in the footer below.

How do I get a refund?

If there is a problem with a class, tell microedu before the class is marked Done and you get a full refund. Your payment stays held safely with microedu until the class is Done, so the money is still there to return. After Done the money is released to the teacher and cannot be refunded. See the full activity flow →

What if the teacher declines my request?

The booking is cancelled and you are notified right away. Pick another teacher from your suggestions, or send a new request — microedu finalises the class with both sides. See the full activity flow →

What if there is a problem during the class?

Raise it with microedu before the class is marked Done: the payment is still held, so you get a full refund, and the other teachers for that help become available again at once. If the class already finished and was marked Done, tell microedu anyway — we will look into it.

How does the time bank work?

Teach a free class and you earn time credit: 1 hour = 60 minutes. Spend that credit learning from teachers who accept time exchange. Money classes are always paid by bKash, never with time credit.

How do I teach on microedu?

Register once — one account for learning and teaching — then complete your full teacher profile from your dashboard; every CV field is required. Once saved, learners can find you through I Need Help suggestions. You set your own rate: hourly, by bidding, or completely free — there is no price ceiling.
